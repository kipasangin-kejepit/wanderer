# Classifier v1 on the red-source survey (before the fix)

v1 decided "methane" from the CH4−M colour (3.35 vs 4.6 µm, relative to field stars).
On the first 9 survey targets (8 measurable, all with NO proper motion, so most likely
distant dusty active galaxies), v1 said:

| Source | v1 label |
|---|---|
| CWISE J021414.62−184548.6 | Y-type / very cold brown dwarf (medium) |
| CWISE J070748.03+590304.2 | Brown dwarf candidate (methane) |
| CWISE J071642.30+631117.2 | Brown dwarf candidate (methane) |
| CWISE J094859.00+092428.2 | Y-type / very cold brown dwarf (high) |
| CWISE J095423.47+065722.1 | Y-type / very cold brown dwarf (high) |
| CWISE J131236.24+330633.9 | Y-type / very cold brown dwarf (high) |
| CWISE J071911.15+574058.1 | Infrared excess (warm dust) |
| CWISE J163647.51+605423.9 | Infrared excess (warm dust) |

**6 of 8 non-moving red sources were wrongly called brown dwarfs.** A steep red power law
makes CH4−M positive just like methane does. v2 measures methane as a trough below the
2.2–3.95 µm continuum and combines the spectrum with catalogue proper motion.
This is why the classifier had to be tested on objects it was not tuned on.
